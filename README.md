🌾 AgriGuard — Crop Insurance Risk Assessment Engine

A web-based multi-factor crop insurance risk assessment system that evaluates agricultural conditions and generates an estimated risk score, risk level, and insurance recommendation.

The system combines crop sensitivity, rainfall, temperature, soil moisture, and scenario-based factors to help users understand potential agricultural risk.

🚀 Features

- 🌱 Crop-specific risk assessment
- 🌧️ Rainfall risk analysis
- 🌡️ Temperature stress analysis
- 💧 Soil moisture assessment
- 📊 Weighted 0–100 risk scoring
- 🛡️ Insurance plan recommendations
- 🔄 Scenario simulation for drought, rainfall reduction, and pest outbreaks
- 📈 Risk factor contribution charts
- 📊 Risk distribution and trend visualization
- 🤖 Gemini-powered AgriBot assistant

⚙️ How It Works

Farm & Environmental Inputs
          ↓
Crop Sensitivity Analysis
          ↓
Rainfall + Temperature + Soil Moisture Analysis
          ↓
Weighted Risk Scoring
          ↓
Scenario Impact
          ↓
Final Risk Score
          ↓
Low / Medium / High Risk
          ↓
Insurance Recommendation

🧮 Risk Factors

Factor| Weight
Rainfall| 30%
Temperature| 25%
Soil Moisture| 25%
Crop Sensitivity| 20%

The system uses predefined agricultural thresholds and weighted scoring rules to calculate the estimated risk.

🔄 Scenario Simulator

Users can test how different conditions may affect the assessment:

- Normal
- Drought
- Rainfall Reduction
- Pest Outbreak

This allows users to explore how environmental scenarios can change the overall risk level.

🤖 AgriBot

AgriGuard includes an AI assistant powered by the Google Gemini API.

AgriBot can answer questions related to crop insurance risk, environmental factors, risk scores, and agricultural risk management.
